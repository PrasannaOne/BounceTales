BOUNCE'S FIELD GUIDE

How to Play

◀ / ▶ Move Bounce across the level.

JUMP Bounce from the ground and obstacles. Hold for the normal jump action.

* Change form when a new form has been unlocked. Some obstacles need a particular form.

Eggs Collect them, find checkpoints and discover the level's hidden routes.

PC A/D or arrows move · Space jumps · Shift or Q changes form · Escape pauses. Standard gamepads work too.

Keyboard: A / D or ← / → move · Space jump · Shift form · Esc pause
